About

My work moves between interpretation and implementation. I combine qualitative and quantitative research with bibliometrics, natural-language processing, data visualisation, and web development. My substantive interests include digital governance, science and innovation policy, generative AI, political representation, and critical theory.

I work as a project manager and researcher at the Centre for Social Innovation (ZSI) in Vienna and study mathematics alongside political science.

Research

Two longer studies trace a line from algorithmic governance to generative systems and the forms of resistance they invite.

M.A. thesis · University of Vienna

Nomadic Descent

Generative AI, Subjectivation, and Resistance/Critique in Control Societies. A study of generative AI through post-structuralist political theory and the problem of subjectivation under control.

B.A. thesis · University of Vienna

From Panopticon to Palantír

Algorithmic Governance in the Post-Disciplinary Societies. An inquiry into the shift from disciplinary institutions toward continuous, data-driven modulation.
